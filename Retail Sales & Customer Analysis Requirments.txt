A German based online retail company sells unique all occasion giftware. The customers of the company are retail as well as wholesalers.

The company has collected data containing all the transactions that took place between 2009 and 2011. The dataset contains eight attributes or columns. They are:

1. The invoice number attribute is a six-digit number. It is a unique value assigned to a transaction. If an invoice number begins with the letter ‘c’, it means that the particular invoice was cancelled.

2. The stock code attribute is the product item code. It is a unique five-digit integral number assigned to a product.

3. The description attribute is the name of a product item.

4. The quantity attribute is the total quantity of each product for a particular transaction.

5. The invoice date attribute contains the day and time when a specific transaction took place.

6. The unit price attribute is the price of one unit of a product in sterling.

7. The customer ID attribute is a unique five-digit integral number assigned to a customer.

8. The country attribute is the name of the country where a customer resides.

Your goal, as a data analyst, is to conduct data transformation, modelling, and visualizations to answer questions that can influence business decisions. The questions to which the company needs answer to are:

- Which countries had the highest and lowest product demands?

- Which products were the highest and lowest contributors to revenue generation?

- Is there any pattern in the demand for the products as per weather conditions? Consider four weather conditions, namely, Spring, from March to May; Summer, from June to August; Autumn, from September to November; and Winter, from December to February.

- Who are the top customers in terms of contribution to revenue?

- What is the rate of repeat customers? The repeat customer rate is calculated by dividing the number of return customers by the total number of customers and multiplying by 100.
