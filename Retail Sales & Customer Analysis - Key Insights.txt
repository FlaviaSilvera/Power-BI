🚀 Retail Sales & Customer Analytics Dashboard | Power BI ,DAX, Excel, Power Query Project

This project focused on transforming large-scale raw data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making, performance tracking, and customer behavior analysis.

📊 Project Overview:
Worked with a dataset of 541,910 rows and 16 columns, where I performed data cleaning, transformation, and modeling to build an interactive and dynamic dashboard using Power BI,DAX, Excel, Power Query.

Key areas of analysis included:

Sales and profit performance analysis
Customer behavior and repeat purchase analysis
Seasonal trends and demand forecasting insights
Country-wise sales distribution analysis

📌 Key Business Insights:

💰 Total Revenue: $9.75M
📈 Total Profit: $5.83K
🔁 Repeat Customers: 541.91K
🌍 United Kingdom emerged as the top-performing country in sales
🍂 Highest product demand observed during Autumn season

⚠️ Business Problem Identified:

Significant sales disparity across countries
Low customer retention indicating a critical customer loyalty gap

💡 Recommendations:

Implement customer loyalty and rewards programs
Introduce personalized email marketing campaigns
Develop localized marketing strategies by region
Offer targeted discounts to encourage repeat purchases

🛠 Tools & Technologies Used:

Power BI (Dashboard Development & Visualization)
DAX (Advanced Calculations & KPIs)
Power Query (Data Transformation)
Excel (Data Preparation)
Data Modeling & Business Intelligence
