Retail Sales & Customer Analysis Dashboard

📊 Project Overview:
Worked with a dataset of 541,910 rows and 16 columns, where I performed data cleaning, transformation, and modeling to build an interactive and dynamic dashboard using Power BI,DAX, Excel, Power Query.

Key areas of analysis included:

Sales and profit performance analysis
Customer behavior and repeat purchase analysis
Seasonal trends and demand forecasting insights
Country-wise sales distribution analysis

📌 Key Business Insights:

💰 Total Revenue: $9.75M
📈 Total Profit: $5.83K
🔁 Repeat Customers: 541.91K
🌍 United Kingdom emerged as the top-performing country in sales
🍂 Highest product demand observed during Autumn season
